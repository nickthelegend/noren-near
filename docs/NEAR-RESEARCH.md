# NEAR integration research (2026-11-09 UTC)

This document records live observations, not a promise that every route is executable. `GET /v0/tokens` returned 216 entries when checked. A token listing is not proof of quote liquidity.

## Intents and custody

- [1Click request flow](https://docs.near-intents.org/integration/distribution-channels/1click-api/quickstart/making-a-request.md): `ORIGIN_CHAIN` quote returns a quote-specific deposit address; poll `/v0/status` and require `SUCCESS`. `dry: true` only validates/quotes and does not move funds. The API has no testnet.
- [Verifier deposits](https://docs.near-intents.org/integration/verifier-contract/deposits-and-withdrawals/deposits.md) and [balances](https://docs.near-intents.org/integration/verifier-contract/deposits-and-withdrawals/balances.md): `intents.near` holds NEP-141/245 balances under an internal account. Its balance view is `mt_balance_of`. The contract account can call [direct `ft_withdraw`](https://docs.near-intents.org/integration/verifier-contract/deposits-and-withdrawals/withdrawals.md) to pull its own balance into a NEAR token account. However, a 1Click quote whose recipient is the shared contract account does not pass a borrower ID to `noren_credit`. A status response is off-chain and cannot by itself authorize on-chain credit. We therefore do **not** credit a borrower from a quote ID, deposit address, memo, or unconfirmed balance. The safe present flow quotes to the borrower's Intents account, withdraws to the borrower's NEAR account, then uses NEP-141 `ft_transfer_call` into Noren; the token callback authenticates `sender_id`. Direct contract-recipient quote attribution remains blocked pending a verifiable per-user receipt design.
- [1Click Earn](https://docs.near-intents.org/integration/distribution-channels/1click-api/earn.md) lists receipt assets `gtUSDCp` (Base), `TLO` (Ethereum), and `kV-gtSOLb` (Solana). The 1Click list contained all three. These are third-party vaults and do not automatically make Noren collateral productive.
- [Confidential swaps](https://docs.near-intents.org/integration/distribution-channels/1click-api/quickstart/confidential-swaps.md) has a documented developer path: `confidentiality: basic|advanced` for external-chain swaps, and `CONFIDENTIAL_INTENTS` for embedded balances with signed intents. This does not hide Noren's public NEAR credit position. A shielded ZEC sender can conceal the source in Zcash, while the 1Click deposit address, amount, arrival, and NEAR-side position may remain observable or correlatable. No privacy guarantee is made for Noren spending.

## Live assets and stocks

The 1Click [token endpoint](https://1click.chaindefuser.com/v0/tokens) contained `nep141:btc.omft.near` (BTC), `nep141:zec.omft.near` (ZEC), `nep141:eth.omft.near` (ETH), `nep141:sol.omft.near` (SOL), NEAR `wBTC` (`2260fac5e5542a773aa44fbcfedf7c193bc2c599.factory.bridge.near`), `nbtc.bridge.near`, `eth.bridge.near`, and native NEAR USDC (`17208628f84f5d6ad33f0da3bbbeb27ffcb398eac501a31bd6ad2011e36133a1`). There were **no** NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, SPY, or xStocks symbol matches. Robinhood Chain `ETH` is listed, but no stock token is. Tokenised stocks are unreachable through this observed 1Click catalog; they are not enabled as collateral. The list must be rechecked because availability can change.

## Pricing and yield

- [Pyth's NEAR guide](https://docs.pyth.network/price-feeds/core/use-real-time-data/pull-integration/near) says Pyth Core ceased NEAR support on 2026-08-26. Pyth equity feeds cannot presently secure a NEAR-native stock collateral contract.
- Rhea/Burrow's [mainnet price oracle](https://guide.rhea.finance/near-chain-guides/lending-and-borrowing/how-lending-and-borrowing-works/oracle) is `priceoracle.near`; [OutLayer's on-demand oracle](https://github.com/out-layer/oracle-example) uses `price-oracle.near` and `oracle_on_call`, with `price-oracle-wrapper.testnet` for testing. A cached price requires explicit age validation. The latter does not document equity feeds. Contract prices must come from the configured oracle account, with a freshness bound.
- Rhea lending is `contract.main.burrow.near` ([SDK](https://github.com/rhea-finance/lending-sdk-golang)). Its live [token-data endpoint](https://api.burrow.finance/list_token_data) returned the following **supply APYs**, observed 2026-11-09 ~14:55 UTC: WBTC bridge 1.03%; NBTC 0.49%; ETH bridge 1.00%; USDC 10.27%; USDC.e 9.50%; ZEC 0.08%; wNEAR 0.34%. The endpoint listed no SOL market. These are rates at observation, may change, and are not Noren earnings. The relevant token IDs are the exact Rhea market tokens, which differ from some 1Click origin asset IDs. Supply, receipt/share accounting, unwind, and yield-only extraction require an integrated adapter and live verification before self-repayment can be claimed.
- [rNEAR staking SDK](https://github.com/rhea-finance/rnear-sdk) identifies `lst.rhealab.near` mainnet and `lst.ref-dev.testnet` testnet; staking creates rNEAR shares and an approximately 30-hour delayed unstake route. It only earns on NEAR, not on BTC/ZEC/ETH/SOL.

## Mainnet boundaries

1Click, `intents.near`, Rhea lending, and merchant settlement are mainnet-only. A dry quote is evidence of live API connectivity, not a swap. A full live loan loop requires a funded `NEAR_MAINNET_ACCOUNT`, collateral on its origin chain, pool USDC, a deployed and audited Noren contract, and explicit mainnet transaction authorization. No such loop is claimed here.
