| Mainnet loop | Nick supplies a funded `NEAR_MAINNET_ACCOUNT`, origin-chain collateral and refund wallet, 1 USDC of real pool liquidity plus fees, and a Base/Arbitrum merchant recipient he controls. Mainnet transactions require separate explicit authorization. | Real 1Click deposit, borrow, 1 USDC merchant payment, harvest, repay and return-to-origin withdrawal. | Safe direct 1Click recipient attribution | Engineering work: a verifiable receipt or per-borrower account scheme that passes a confirmed settlement and borrower identity to the contract.
